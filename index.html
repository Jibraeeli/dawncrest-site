The Infrastructure

Don't sell. Route.

The model sits between demand and supply: read market signals, identify timing windows, and connect the right people before the noise does.

01 · Signal

Signal Detection

Funding rounds. Key hires. Regulatory citations. Expansion moves. Every connection starts with a verified signal, not a guess.

02 · Routing

Precision Routing

Introductions are timed and deliberate, so both sides arrive ready. No volume games.

03 · Access

Selective Access

Access is the product. The value is proximity to the network and the timing of each introduction, not promises or volume.

How It Works

Three steps. No complexity.

Step 01

Identify the signal

A funding round closes. A VP of Engineering is hired. A company posts five roles in a week. The aim is to spot it before the market does.

Step 02

Route the introduction

Both sides are briefed. Context is set. The introduction lands warm, with timing and relevance already built in.

Step 03

Hand over the conversation

The connector steps back. The relationship belongs to both sides, with no one sitting in on calls or chasing follow-ups.
